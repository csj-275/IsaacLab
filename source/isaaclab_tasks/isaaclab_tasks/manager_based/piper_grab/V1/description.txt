13. 任务流程图 (V1 两步抓放)
================================================================================

    Reset → [Step 1: 抓取 object_1 (方块)]
                   ↓ grasp_1 完成
            [Step 2: 放入 box]
                   ↓ placed_1 完成
            [Step 3: 抓取 mug (马克杯)]
                   ↓ grasp_2 完成
            [Step 4: 放入 box]
                   ↓ 夹爪释放
            [SUCCESS: object_1 + mug 都在 box 内]

    子任务检测参数:
      grasp_1:  |eef_pos - object_1_pos| < 0.15 m  (object_grasped)
      placed_1: |object_1_xy - box_xy| < 0.08 m AND |object_1_z - box_z| < 0.08 m
      grasp_2:  |eef_pos - mug_pos| < 0.15 m
      success:  |object_1_xy - box_xy| < 0.08 m AND |object_1_z - box_z| < 0.1 m
            AND |mug_xy - box_xy| < 0.08 m AND |mug_z - box_z| < 0.1 m
            AND gripper_open = True

    中间失败:
      - object_1 或 mug Z < -0.05 (掉落)
      - 30s 超时
